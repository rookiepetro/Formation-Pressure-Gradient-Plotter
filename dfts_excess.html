📖 User Manual: How to Use Excess Pressure Pro
1. Overview

This tool uses the Alton Brown "Excess Pressure" method to remove the hydrostatic gradient from your pressure data. This allows you to visualize fluid density changes, compartments, and contacts with high precision.

2. Loading Data
  • Prepare your data in Excel: Column A = Depth (m), Column B = Pressure (psi).
  • Copy both columns (without headers).
  • Paste them into the "Paste Depth(m) & Press(psi)..." box in the sidebar.
  • Click "Load Data".
3. Defining Zones & Calculating Density
  1. In the "Zone Definition" table, select the points belonging to a specific fluid leg (e.g., Water Zone).
  2. Adjust the Ref Density Slider until the selected points form a Vertical Line on the chart.
  3. Alternatively, type the known density (e.g., 1.03) into the input box.
  4. Enter a name (e.g., "Water Leg") and click "Draw Extended Line".
  5. The app calculates the exact density and projects a dashed line.
4. Finding Fluid Contacts

Repeat Step 3 for the Oil Zone. Adjust the density until the oil points are vertical (usually around 0.6-0.8 g/cc). The intersection of the extended Water Line and Oil Line represents the Free Water Level (FWL).
